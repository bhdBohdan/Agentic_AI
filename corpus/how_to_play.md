# Basics of Battle — Clash Royale Wiki

## Overview & Win Conditions

Players win a battle by obtaining more **Crowns** than their opponent before time runs out. Crowns are earned by destroying the opponent's Crown Towers. 

* **Instant Victory:** Destroying the opponent's **King's Tower** immediately awards **3 Crowns** and automatically destroys the remaining Crown Towers, ending the match instantly.
* **Normal Match Length:** 3 minutes of standard play, followed by Overtime (Sudden Death) if tied.

### How a Battle Ends:
1. **3 Crowns Achieved:** A player destroys the King's Tower or all 3 enemy towers.
2. **Time Expires (Regular Time):** A player has more Crowns than their opponent at the end of the 3-minute regulation time.
3. **Overtime (Sudden Death):** The first player to destroy any Crown Tower in Overtime wins immediately.
4. **Tiebreaker:** If no tower is destroyed by the end of Overtime, a Tiebreaker is triggered.

> **Tiebreaker Mechanism:** All remaining Crown Towers rapidly lose hitpoints simultaneously until the single tower with the lowest health is destroyed. 
> * If all towers have identical health at the end of the tiebreaker, the match ends in a **Draw** (neither player gains or loses trophies).
> * In Clan Wars Duels, if a match ends in a true tie after the tiebreaker, a **coin flip** decides the winner.

## Trophies, Arenas & Rewards

* **Trophies:** Winning multiplayer battles awards Trophies, while losing subtracts Trophies.
* **Arenas & Leagues:** Accumulating Trophies unlocks higher Arenas and Leagues, granting access to new cards, higher victory gold, increased card donation/request limits, and Trophy Road rewards.
* **Challenges & Tournaments:** 
  * **Special Event / Victory Challenges:** Offer one-time milestone rewards up to a maximum win limit.
  * **Global Tournaments:** Continue indefinitely until match losses run out.

## Battle Mechanics & Territory

Battles take place on a battle arena featuring **6 buildings** (3 per player: 2 Princess/Crown Towers and 1 King's Tower).

* **Deck & Hand:** Players battle using a deck of **8 Cards**.
* **Territory:** 
  * Cards are placed on the player's side of the arena (the non-red area ending at the river/bridge).
  * Most Spells and select cards (e.g., Miner, Goblin Barrel) can be placed anywhere on the field.
  * **Captured Territory:** Destroying an enemy Crown Tower expands the player's deployable territory slightly past the bridge on that side. *(Note: Royal Recruits require both opposing towers to be destroyed before they can be placed on the opponent's side).*
* **Emotes:** Players can communicate using animated Emotes or quick text messages ("Thanks!", "Good Game!"). Emotes can be muted using the speech bubble toggle menu.

## Elixir Management

**Elixir** is the resource required to play cards. It regenerates automatically over the course of the match.

* **Elixir Advantage:** Occurs when a player has more available Elixir than their opponent.
* **Positive Elixir Trade:** Defending an opponent's attack using less Elixir than the opponent spent to launch it. 
* **Defensive Advantage:** Crown Towers assist in defense for free, giving defenders an inherent Elixir efficiency advantage. To overcome this, attackers must construct large pushes or capitalize on when the opponent is out of counter-cards.

## Battle Deck Construction & Slots

A Battle Deck consists of **8 main Cards** plus **1 Tower Troop slot**.

### Deck Slot Breakdown:
| Slot | Type / Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slot 1** | **Evolution Slot** | Enables Card Evolution when cycle conditions are met. |
| **Slot 2** | **Heroic Slot** | Enables Heroic capabilities via Hero Shards. |
| **Slot 3** | **Wild Card Slot** | Can be configured as either Evolved or Heroic. |
| **Slot 9** | **Tower Troop** | Determines the main defense capability and stats of the Crown Towers (e.g., Cannoneer, Princess). |

* **Champions:** Up to **2 Champion cards** can be included in a deck, but they can only occupy the Heroic or Wild Card slots.
* **Saved Decks:** Players can save up to **3 decks** initially, expanding up to **5 decks** at Level 7 and **10 decks** at Level 17.

## Card Rotation & Cycling

1. **Starting Hand:** At the start of a match, 4 cards are randomly drawn into the hand, with a 5th card placed in the **"Next"** queue.
   * *Exceptions:* **Elixir Collector** and **Mirror** will never appear in the initial starting hand.
2. **Card Replacement Cooldown:** Playing a card moves the "Next" card into the hand with a **2-second delay** (reduced to 1 second during Double Elixir).
3. **Cycle Rule:** Once a card is played, it requires playing **3 other cards** before it reappears in the hand rotation.
4. **Evolutions:** Evolving cards require playing their standard version a set number of times (cycles) before triggering the Evolved form. *(Mirroring a card does not count toward Evolution cycle progression).*

## Combat & Damage Rules

* **Targeting & Sight Range:** Troops automatically lock onto and engage the nearest valid target within their sight range. If no targets are nearby, troops advance toward the nearest enemy Crown Tower.
* **Buildings:** Stationary structures with a decaying lifetime bar. They continuously lose hitpoints over time even if unattacked.
* **Damage Types:**
  * **Point Damage:** Single-target damage.
  * **Area Damage (AoE):** Damages multiple targets within a radius simultaneously.
  * **Chain Damage:** Hits a primary target and bounces to adjacent targets.
* **Friendly Fire & Buffs:** 
  * Spells and negative effects (freeze, slow, pushback) **do not damage or affect friendly units**.
  * Positive effects (Rage, Heal, Clone) **only apply to friendly units**.
