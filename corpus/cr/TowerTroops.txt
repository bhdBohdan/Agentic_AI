Tower Troops are troops that defend the Crown Towers. The player can choose their Tower Troop before going into battle. Just like troop, spell and building cards, Tower Troop cards have rarities, and can be upgraded to a maximum level of 16, however, it cannot be upgraded past the player's King's Tower level even if it's under the starting level. The player can get Tower Troop cards through Lucky Chests or offers from the Shop. When upgrading to a new King Level, the player will get enough cards and gold to upgrade their Tower Princess.

General information
Each player has a pair of 3×3 size Crown Towers on their respective side of the Arena, one on the left and one on the right of the King's Tower.
The Crown Towers are classified as a building and will be targeted by building-targeting troops.
Destroying an opposing Crown Tower awards a player a Crown and grants the player additional territory across the river (commonly called a “pocket”) where cards can be cast upon (the Royal Recruits card needs both Crown Towers to be destroyed to be deployed in the pocket)
There are certain cards that will deal reduced damage to Crown Towers. More specifically:
The Evolved Firecracker's Sparks will deal 6-7% of their full damage to Crown Towers.
The Hero Ballon ability will deal 10-11% of their full damage.
The Log and Void's single target will deal 13-14% of their full damage.
Void's 2-4 targets beams will deal 17-18% of their full damage.
The Miner and Arrows will deal 20-21% of their full damage.
Void's 5+ targets beam, Poison and Vines will deal 22-23% of their full damage.
Rocket and Fireball will deal 23-24% of their full damage.
Evolved Dart Goblin's Minimum Poison Damage and Hero Berserker ability will also deal 23-24% of their full damage.
The Goblinstein's ability and Evolved Dart Goblin's Maximum Poison Damage damage will deal 24-25% of their full damage.
Zap, Giant Snowball, Rage, Freeze and Lightning will deal 25-26% of their full damage.
Due to spawning a Rage after death, the Lumberjack's death Rage will also deal 25-26% of its full damage to Crown Towers.
The Evolved Cannon's Barrage Damage will also deal 25-26% of their full damage.
The Goblin Curse will deal 28-29% of its full damage.
The Tornado will deal 29-30% of its full damage.
The Goblin Machine's Rocket and Hero Wizard ability will deal 33-34% of its full damage.
The Evolved Valkyrie's Tornado will deal 44% of its full damage.
The Hero Valkyrie Ability will deal 49-50% of their full damage.
The Electro Giant's Reflected Damage and Hero Barbarian Barrel ability will deal 50-51% of their full damage.
The Earthquake will deal 60-61% of its full troop damage.
The Tower Troops' attack speed can be temporarily increased by a Rage spell like all other active buildings and troops.
Similarly, the Tower Troops' attack speed can be temporarily decreased by a Giant Snowball, Ice Golem and Ice Wizard, like all other active buildings and troops.

The Baby Goblins was a temporary Common card that were unlocked from Goblin Mines (Goblins 1). It was a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged tower troop with high hitpoints and low damage. Additionally, it would give an ability to the Goblin Queen Tower. They appear as smaller versions of Goblins wearing goggles, a silver earring, and pale brown underpants, have khaki and orange hair, and carry daggers and slingshot leather pads.

Strategy
The Baby Goblins is a well-rounded tower troop as it can handle both many swarms and tanks decently well.
The Baby Goblins is the tower troop tied with the Tower Princess for the most health. This will make it better against siege decks or spell cycle, since it can tank more hits.
After a Crown Tower has already been destroyed, if a melee troop, such as Knight, Hog Rider, Royal Ghost, Valkyrie, Miner, Golem, or Giant, is attacking your Goblin Queen on the opposite side of your other Baby Goblin, your Baby Goblin will aid the Goblin Queen in taking out the threat.
However, if the melee troop has a Long melee range (1.6 tiles) and is attacking the Goblin Queen from low on the opposite side, the remaining Baby Goblin will not help the Goblin Queen as the troop would be just out of range. This is important to keep in mind for high DPS units that could easily threaten your Goblin Queen, such as P.E.K.K.A, Three Musketeers, Inferno Dragon, Evolved Royal Recruits, Mighty Miner, Prince, Mini P.E.K.K.A, Elite Barbarians, Sparky, and Night Witch.
The Baby Goblins has an attack range that is 0.5 tiles longer and a hit speed that is 0.2 seconds faster than the Goblin Queen, however, their projectiles are slightly slower.
Against heavy tanks, such as Golem and Giant, defensive buildings that have a 3x3 deploy area should be placed in the 3-4 position. This will lure the Golem/Giant to the building instead of the Crown Towers while allowing for both of the Baby Goblins to target the Golem/Giant, destroying them much faster.
Buildings that have a 2x2 deploy area, like Tesla, should be placed in the 3-3 position as 3-4 will usually not pull them to the building.
