### Positive RAG Questions

- **1.** How long does regular Touchdown gameplay last?
  - _Three minutes. If the score is tied, overtime lasts up to two minutes or until one side earns a Crown._
- **2.** How does Elixir generation work in regular 2v2 battles?
  - _It is reduced to 85% of normal generation._
- **3.** How many main Cards are in a standard Clash Royale battle deck?
  - _Eight main Cards, plus one Tower Troop slot._
- **4.** What is the main goal of a Bait deck?
  - _Force the opponent to use a counter, then attack with the win condition while that counter is out of cycle._
- **5.** What should a player choose first when building a deck?
  - _One or two win conditions that will mainly deal damage to enemy towers._
- **6.** What defensive cards should a deck-building guide include for air units?
  - _At least two cards that can deal with air units, such as Musketeer, Archers, or Baby Dragon._
- **7.** What should a player do when their opening hand contains only risky cards?
  - _Wait for the opponent to make the first move and react accordingly._
